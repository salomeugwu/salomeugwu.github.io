01 / ABOUT ME

I’ve always been drawn to people.

Growing up in a populated environment in Lagos, I learned early that people respond to pressure, conflict, and difficult situations in very different ways. Some people need clear answers, some need patience, and sometimes people simply need someone who will listen and understand them. That experience made me observant, patient, and comfortable meeting people where they are.

I also grew up around large family businesses, which gave me another perspective. I saw that running a successful business is not only about selling a product or making money. It is also about the people who trust you, ask questions, need reassurance, and sometimes need someone to solve a problem for them. That made me appreciate just how important good customer care is to a business.

Interestingly, I didn't always see myself going into customer support. Growing up, I wanted to become a doctor because I wanted to help stop people's pain. As I grew older, however, I realized that what really drew me to that idea was the human side of it — listening to people, understanding what they were going through, and being able to make things a little better.

At one point, I told my mother that I wanted to work in Customer Success because I wanted a career where I would interact with and help people. Like many parents, she initially imagined more traditional career paths for me. But I came to understand that customer support professionals can be just as important to a business. A company can have a great product, but if customers don't feel heard, supported, or valued, that trust can quickly disappear.

That is why I chose to pursue customer support.

As a Business Administration graduate from Lagos State University, I bring together my understanding of business with my natural strengths in communication, empathy, patience, attention to detail, and problem-solving. I'm currently looking for an opportunity where I can learn from experienced professionals, contribute to a team, and grow into a customer support professional who genuinely makes a difference.

I'm at the beginning of my professional journey, but I know exactly why I chose this path — I want to help people and be part of the reason they have a better experience with a business.
